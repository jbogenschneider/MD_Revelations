# Robot equipment requirements

Requirements begin when the corresponding technology is researched, not automatically on the calendar year. Quantities are per battalion/company/HQ. Later robot models satisfy the same archetype requirement; 2035 does not add a second requirement.

The tactical drone company gains 120 land drones from the 2015 land_Drone_equipment3 technology. Its base land-drone requirement was removed to avoid duplication. Both drone companies have their base robot requirements moved to the 2025 robot technology. Drone recon retains its base land-drone requirement.
